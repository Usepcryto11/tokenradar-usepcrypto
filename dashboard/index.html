Token Radar

Every protocol that records no token and is a launch candidate, ranked by what its token would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is. This is the entry's own claim — not a prediction of whether or when a token is issued.

How the value is scored model radar-2

implied value = TVL × 0.35 × category × growth × maturity × breadth

  • TVL × 0.35 — established DeFi governance tokens commonly trade at a fully-diluted value of roughly a fifth to a half of TVL; 0.35 is the middle of that band.
  • Category — 1.00 normally; 0.60 for a wrapped, staked or bridged product (a receipt for an asset that already exists, so a new token captures less new value); 0.80 for a centralised exchange; 0.90 when no category is recorded; ×0.75 more when the name ends in a version marker. Exchanges and wrapped, staked or bridged products are not ranked at all — except the two the watchlist itself names — because their TVL is custodial balances or a claim on an asset that already exists; they are listed below, with their reasons.
  • Growth — 1 + clamp(7-day TVL change, −50%, +150%) ÷ 100 × 0.50. One week's swing is noisy, so the extremes are clamped; an unreported change counts as 0.
  • Maturity — 0.55 + min(age, 1095 days) ÷ 1095 × 0.45. A protocol that has run for years with no token has proven demand; a brand-new one is unproven. Age unknown: the snapshot's own median pre-token age (516 days).
  • Breadth — 1.00 on one chain, 1.05 on two or three, 1.10 on four or more. Multi-chain deployment is a weak signal of real usage, worth a tenth at most.

No protocol name is ever special-cased: the same formula scores every row, from the snapshot's own figures alone. A protocol is called pre-token only because its record carries no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol — the record is not proof it has no token.
